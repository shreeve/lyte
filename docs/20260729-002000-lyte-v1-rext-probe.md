# V-1 — the NVENC Rext 4:4:4 probe (2026-07-29)

*H4 wave 0, slice V-1 (`docs/20260728-194226-lyte-h4-plan.md` §2). Every
host-side **[probe]** mark in the plan's §1, answered with a number on
the reference host: pup, RTX 4050 Laptop GPU, driver 595.84, Ubuntu
26.04, ffmpeg/libavcodec 8.0.1-3ubuntu2, offline encode legs only (no
capture, no wire, no session). Instrument: `lyte-encode-check` grown
pix-fmt/profile/rgb-mode knobs on the C leaf's loud-reject pattern +
`Host/Scripts/rext-probe.sh` (this report's tables are its output,
verbatim in `pup:~/rext-probe-run.log`; parseable rows in
`pup:~/rext-probe/results.tsv`). All encodes 2048×1280@60, preset p4,
capped-CQ cq12 / cap 50 Mbps — the shipped session posture. Encodes
measured deterministic: the interrupted first run and the full second
run produced bit-identical PSNR to six decimals. Bitstreams for V-2
kept at `pup:~/rext-probe/keep/` (11 files: {desk,motion,bars} ×
{p420,gbrp,rgbmode,conv}, minus bars-p420 which V-2 doesn't need).*

The three 4:4:4 input paths, named as the plan names them:

- **gbrp** — planar RGB in → Rext identity-matrix full-range. Zero
  matrix conversion anywhere; the leaf repacks capture's packed BGRx
  to G/B/R planes (the production shape, measured below).
- **rgbmode** — packed bgr0 in + `rgb_mode=yuv444` → the driver
  converts internally; nvenc.c forces the VUI to BT.601 limited.
- **conv** — host-side BT.709 full-range YUV444 conversion, then
  planar yuv444p in. The probe stands the future conversion leaf in
  with offline swscale; the encode leg signs exactly that conversion.
- **p420** — today's shipped path (bgr0 → NVENC internal 4:2:0),
  raced as the baseline.

## Q1 — does Rext open on this box, and is `rgb_mode` there?

**RESULT q1: YES on every count.** `-profile rext` opens through the
production C leaf on all three 4:4:4 input paths (a successful open IS
the `NV_ENC_CAPS_SUPPORT_YUV444_ENCODE` caps check — the wrapper
rejects at open when the cap reads 0). `rgb_mode` is on pup's
libavcodec option surface with the `yuv444` value. Bonus fact for V-4:
the wrapper **auto-selects Rext for gbrp input even without
`-profile`** (bitstream says Rext regardless) — we still set it
explicitly, declaration over inference.

```
gbrp:    opens — bitstream says: Rext gbrp gbr/pc
rgbmode: opens — bitstream says: Rext yuv444p bt470bg/tv
conv:    opens — bitstream says: Rext yuv444p bt709/pc
```

4:4:4 paths converge (46.6–47.0 dB, rate-limited at the 50 cap) over
p420's 33.4 dB. PSNR is measured in RGB against the raw BGRx reference
with each stream decoded under its claimed interpretation (the honest
cross-path metric; the claim itself is verified below).

The plan's §1 claims are now measurements: gbrp is mathematically
exact RGB (identity, full range, honored); packed-RGB `rgb_mode` is
forced 601-limited on the 4:4:4 path exactly as on Stage A's 4:2:0
path; a host-side BT.709-full conversion round-trips with honest
signing. The C leaf now signs each path's truth (identity/full for
gbrp, 709/full for yuv444p input, Stage A's 601-limited for packed).

**Owner decision 2 evidence (preference order).** The recommended
order **gbrp → host-conversion → rgb_mode** holds on the host-side
numbers: gbrp dominates quality (+5.5 dB over both alternatives on
text, at moderate bitrate) and is the only mathematically-exact path;
conv and rgbmode are quality-equivalent to each other (47.8 vs 48.2 dB
text, 47.0 vs 46.9 motion) so the conversion leaf buys full-range
*without* a quality edge over the free driver path — if gbrp fails
V-2's render test, the real fight is "conversion leaf's build cost vs
601-limited-for-free". Final ruling awaits V-2's client half
(identity/GBR full-range render is gbrp's make-or-break), co-signed
into this report.

## Q3 — 4:4:4 throughput at 2048×1280@60 p4

**RESULT q3: every path sustains 60 fps with margin. Worst case is
gbrp at 99–103 fps capacity (10.0 ms/frame mean, of which 4.4 ms is
the CPU-side repack — encoder share ~5.6 ms ≈ 180 fps). conv runs
170–180 fps (5.5–5.9 ms), rgbmode 133–139 fps (7.2–7.5 ms), both
FASTER than the p420 baseline at this geometry (137–145 fps, 6.9–7.3
ms — NVENC's internal RGB→420 conversion costs more than encoding
pre-converted 4:4:4).** p99s: 6.4–11.9 ms, all inside the frame
period. Context from the beauty bar's finding: the SESSION pipeline
ingests ~48/s under full load at 4:2:0 — the encoder is not the
binding constraint there and would not become it at 4:4:4; these
numbers are the encoder's share of that budget, measured clean.

p1 fallback ladder (motion, the plan's named fallback before any
resolution talk): gbrp 8.67 ms / 115 fps, rgbmode 6.13 ms / 163 fps,
conv 4.69 ms / 213 fps — p1 buys ~1.3–1.5 ms/frame if ever needed.

Capped-CQ behavior at 4:4:4 (the ratchet contract): identical posture
to 4:2:0 on every path — QP walks straight to 12 and holds,
byte-stable by frame ≤25 of 300, one IDR, and the static keepalives
are CHEAPER than 4:2:0's at this geometry: 141 B (gbrp) / 163
(rgbmode) / 160 (conv) vs 208 (p420), all at converged QP 12.

## Q4 — IDR sizes vs the 223,380 B FEC ceiling (owner decision 3's evidence)

**RESULT q4: at the 50 Mbps recipe on the text corpus, VBV-uncapped
natural demand mints ceiling-crossing IDRs ROUTINELY — 20 of 30 forced
4:4:4 IDRs (67%) exceed 223,380 B (p50 263–397 KB, max 572–720 KB
by path); the 4:2:0 baseline already crosses at 15/30 (p50 210 KB, max
499 KB). Non-IDR deltas never come close: p50 17–28 KB, max 130–145 KB
across all paths.** Method: 1800 frames of the scrolling-text corpus,
IDR forced every 60 (the 6 s corpus loops, so 6 distinct scroll
positions × 5 samples each — percentiles are quantized accordingly);
sizes books at `pup:~/rext-probe/sizes-*.txt`.

```
path     IDRs  p50      p95      max      ≥ceiling  delta-p50  delta-max
p420     30    209,803  499,209  499,209  15/30     50,521     145,325
gbrp     30    397,243  719,512  719,512  20/30     27,779     141,775
rgbmode  30    263,096  572,046  572,046  20/30     17,438     130,412
conv     30    271,094  600,526  600,526  20/30     21,108     131,826
```

Read for decision 3: under the HS-25 posture, essentially **every
Work-mode text IDR will be a ceiling-sized, VBV-conformed frame**
(~24 datagram-ms of wire each at 50 Mbps) — the guard becomes the
routine IDR shape, not a backstop. The deltas stay far clear, so the
exposure is exactly the IDR frequency, which Work mode keeps
structurally rare (infinite GOP: session start, unprotectable-latch
re-anchors, loss recovery). Note this is not a 4:4:4-only phenomenon —
4:2:0 at cq12 already crosses half the time; 4:4:4 raises the
frequency to two-thirds and the mass by ~1.3–1.9×. The owner rules;
the pre-agreed fallback (a Work-specific VBV) remains a one-line
recipe change.

## Q5 — the BGRx→gbrp repack cost

**RESULT q5: 4.34–4.45 ms/frame at 2048×1280 (mean across 360-frame
legs), single-threaded naive C loop inside the production leaf —
~2.4 GB/s packed-in, 26% of one core's 16.7 ms frame period at 60
fps.** Measured by the leaf itself (`lyte_hevc_enc_repack_us_total`),
not estimated. It rides inside `send()` today, so gbrp's enc-mean
includes it; in a pipelined session it overlaps the previous frame's
encode. Headroom if it ever matters: the loop is unvectorized —
SIMD/multithread or a CUDA-side repack are all untouched options.

## Verdicts, one line each

1. **Rext opens** — yes, all three paths, through the production leaf;
   `rgb_mode` present; wrapper auto-selects Rext for 4:4:4 input.
2. **Color race** — gbrp wins text by +5.5 dB over both alternatives
   (+16 dB over 4:2:0 at fewer bits); all three VUI tags measured
   TRUE; conversion leaf shows no quality edge over free rgb_mode,
   only the range win.
3. **Throughput** — 60 fps holds everywhere at p4; worst path 99 fps
   capacity; conv/rgbmode beat today's 4:2:0 encode time.
4. **Ceiling books** — 67% of natural 4:4:4 text IDRs exceed the
   ceiling (p50 ~1.2–1.8× ceiling); deltas never threaten it; the
   HS-25 posture makes every Work IDR ceiling-shaped.
5. **Repack** — 4.4 ms/frame single-core, measured in-leaf; real but
   affordable, and optimizable if gbrp ships.

## V-2 co-sign — the Mac VideoToolbox Rext probe (2026-07-29)

*H4 wave 0, slice V-2. All 11 `keep/` bitstreams through the EXACT
production construction — `AnnexBAccessUnits` → `DecodeUnit` →
`VideoRenderFactory` → CMSampleBuffer — on the reference client (Apple
M5, macOS 26). Instruments, committed at `3f94a0f` as the §7 harness's
client half: `VideoReadbackTap` (a VTDecompressionSession fed the same
factory samples; hardware engagement read from the live session
property, planes dumped for offline referees) and `lyte-cli
decode-probe` (native/BGRA readback + a real `AVSampleBufferDisplayLayer`
window screenshotted for glass truth — the wire-view construction,
verbatim). Referee: ffmpeg 8.1.2 software decode of the same bitstreams
(whose interpretations THIS report proved true above by 20–56 dB), the
Stage A candidate sweep re-run against VideoToolbox's own RGB output
and against the composited window pixels (1:1 texel-to-device-pixel,
sRGB-matched). Glass PSNRs carry a ~40 dB Display-P3 round-trip noise
floor; winners are unambiguous at 18+ dB margins.*

**RESULT v2-hw: every path hardware-decodes — asserted, not assumed.**
All 11 bitstreams, zero failures, decoder property reads hardware; a
`--require-hardware` session (VTVideoDecoderSpecification *Require*)
also opens on Rext 4:4:4 — the M5 needs no software fallback anywhere.

**RESULT v2-chroma: no silent chroma downsample, decoder → glass.**
At the pixel-buffer boundary the hardware decoder's 4:4:4 planes are
BIT-EXACT against ffmpeg's software decode on all three paths (max abs
diff 0 across 60 bars frames each — for gbrp that means the coded
G/B/R planes come back untouched in the Y/Cb/Cr slots). At the glass,
the desk text corpus matches the full-chroma truth at 39.9 dB —
ABOVE the 38.0 dB separation from a simulated chroma-halved
hypothesis (35.9 dB) — so the compositor samples full-resolution
chroma too.

**RESULT v2-color, the make-or-break: gbrp's identity/GBR full-range
FAILS at the glass.** VideoToolbox's own RGB conversion and the
display-layer composite BOTH interpret matrix-0 4:4:4 as **BT.601
full-range** (range honored, matrix ignored): the candidate sweep
crowns bt601-full by ~30 dB over identity on both instruments, and the
bars render as green/magenta garbage on screen. The failure is
interpretation-only — the decoded planes are perfect — and it is
structural: CoreMedia/CoreVideo have no identity/GBR YCbCrMatrix
vocabulary at all (the format description surfaces our matrix as
absent, primaries/transfer/fullRange correct), so there is nothing to
tag and no attachment to fix. Rendering gbrp correctly would mean
abandoning `AVSampleBufferDisplayLayer` for a custom Metal view — not
the production path this probe was chartered to judge.
**The 601-limited and 709-full paths render CORRECT at the glass**,
asserted per the plan: rgbmode wins as bt601-limited and conv as
bt709-full on both instruments, each by 17+ dB over the runner-up.

**THE JOINT CONVERSION-PATH RULING (owner decision 2, co-signed):
gbrp is OUT** — encode-side king (this report, +5.5 dB), client-side
dead (mis-rendered at the glass through the production path). The
preference ladder collapses to its named fallback fight:
**host-conversion (bt709-full) vs rgb_mode (bt601-limited)**, exactly
as forecast above — quality-equivalent host-side (47.8 vs 48.2 dB
text), BOTH proven correct client-side, so the ruling is purely
"conversion leaf's build cost + 5.9 ms/frame conv encode (fastest
path measured!) buys full range" vs "601-limited free today". V-2
adds one client-side datum to that scale: the conv path's VUI
(bt709/pc) round-trips through VideoToolbox and the glass with zero
platform friction. The final pick is the owner's; both roads are
paved.

**Pixel-format notes for V-5 (the decoder→layer audit):** 4:4:4
streams decode to biplanar full-res 4:4:4 CVPixelBuffers — FourCC
`444f` (full-range) / `444v` (video-range), Y plane + interleaved
CbCr at full width (nv24 layout) — vs today's `420v` NV12; plan for
both in any readback/dump seam. BGRA is requestable straight from the
decompression session with the matrix applied correctly for tagged
streams (that IS the mis-map instrument for untagged/identity). The
client-side VUI assert V-5 wants can read
matrix/range/primaries/transfer off the format description
extensions; note identity arrives as matrix ABSENT + fullRange 1 —
absence is the fingerprint, assert accordingly. Suite 167/167 Mac at
`3f94a0f`.
